Privacy policy

Your journal reflections stay in the browser where you wrote them.

What we store

Your reflections, ratings, favourites and weekly goal are stored in your browser's local storage. If you submit the feedback form, your name (if provided), rating and message are sent to our server.

Where it lives

Journal entries are not uploaded to our server and are not shared with other visitors. They remain in the browser profile on the device where you created them. Feedback submitted through the feedback form is stored in the site's server database so the team can review it.

Tracking

No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies are used by this site. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your visit under Google's own policy.

Your control

Delete an entry from My Journal to remove it from that browser. Clearing your browser data also removes locally stored journal entries, favourites and goals. If other people use the same browser profile, they may be able to access its local journal, so use a personal browser profile and device.

Please don't share

Do not enter passwords, financial information, health details or other sensitive information into the site's text boxes.

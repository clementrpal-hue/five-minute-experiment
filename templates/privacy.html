Privacy policy

Short version: we keep only what you write, and you can delete it.

What we store

Your reflections, ratings, favourites, goal and feedback. We never ask for your email, phone number, address or any ID.

Where it lives

Everything is saved in your browser's local storage. When the site's server is running, reflections and feedback are also saved in its SQLite database so they survive a cleared browser.

Tracking

No advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your visit under Google's own policy.

Your control

Delete any entry from My Journal. Clearing your browser data removes the local copy.

Please don't share

Keep passwords, health details and other sensitive information out of the text boxes.
